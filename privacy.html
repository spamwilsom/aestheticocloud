What we collect

This is a static site. It has no accounts, forms, cookies, advertising or analytics scripts of our own, and we do not collect or store personal information through it.

Some interactive pages, such as Spin Music Sound, ask your browser for microphone access so the visuals can respond to sound. Audio is processed live in your browser. It is not recorded, uploaded or stored, and you can refuse or revoke the permission at any time in your browser settings.

Email

If you write to us, we use your message and address only to reply. We do not sell or share them.
