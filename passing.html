Passing

Pick a mask. Take the call. Find out if you pass.

Voice game · In development

Passing is a voice game where you adopt a persona you're not yet (a quant, an art dealer, the calm friend at a hard dinner) and get called into scenes where an AI character tests whether you can carry it. Afterwards you see where you slipped, what landed, and lines worth stealing.

The phone rings, you're on, and there's no script. Practise safely, or get ambushed for real stakes.

So much of social life is performance, and making it a game is funny. The rehearsal is real, too: what you practise here carries into the actual meeting, gallery opening or hard conversation.

Fictional characters only, and an exit is always within reach.
